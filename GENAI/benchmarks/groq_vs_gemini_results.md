# Groq vs Gemini Benchmark Results

| Metric | Groq LLaMA-3 70B | Gemini 1.5 Flash |
|--------|------------------|------------------|
| Latency | 350-700 ms | 1.2-2.1 s |
| Cost | Free | Free tier |
| Explanation Quality | Technical, precise | Friendly, contextual |
| Risk Detection | Good | Better |
| Stability | Very stable | Sometimes throttles |

Conclusion:
Groq = primary inference (speed)
Gemini = secondary reasoning (context)
